Cooling Fan Control Breakout Board

Cal Poly Formula SAE · Oct 2026

I designed and routed a two-layer PCB in Altium to condition the car’s cooling-fan PWM signal, from the LVM to the next set of fans.

Why it matters

Cooling control depends on the signal reaching the fans as intended. I checked the filtering at 100 kHz in LTspice using a Bode plot.
