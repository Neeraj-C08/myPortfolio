Selected work

01 / Circuit design

555 Timer Board

Personal project

I designed a 555 timer PCB in KiCad as a personal project and soldered the board. The circuit pairs adjustable timing with an LED output, bringing the timer, timing components, and power connection into one layout.

Why it matters

This project gave me practice taking a circuit from schematic and PCB layout through hands-on soldering, with an LED that makes the timer’s output visible.

02 / Vehicle electronics

Vehicle State LED PCB

Cal Poly Prototype Vehicles Laboratory (PROVE Lab)

I designed an LED PCB in Altium to communicate the car’s three high-level vehicle states. The layout separates the low-voltage, autonomy, and high-voltage indicators into three channels.

Why it matters

The design objective is to make the indicators visible in broad daylight to anybody near the car, helping people recognize its state for safety.

PCB designAltiumLED drivers
03 / Circuit design

Cooling Fan Control Breakout Board

Cal Poly Formula SAE · Oct 2026

I designed and routed a two-layer PCB in Altium to condition the car’s cooling-fan PWM signal, from the LVM to the next set of fans.

Why it matters

Cooling control depends on the signal reaching the fans as intended. I checked the filtering at 100 kHz in LTspice using a Bode plot.

PCB designAltiumLTspice
